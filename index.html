The ladder

One path, five courses.

Math builds on itself — so sessions do too. Wherever your student is on this ladder, Henry meets them there and works toward the next rung.

Rung 01

Algebra 1

Variables, linear equations, and functions — the foundation everything after this is built on.

Rung 02

Geometry

Proofs, shapes, and spatial reasoning, taught as logic puzzles instead of memorized rules.

Rung 03

Algebra 2

Polynomials, logarithms, and complex numbers — where most students need a steady second voice.

Rung 04

Precalculus

Trigonometry and sequences, with an eye toward the way of thinking calculus will demand.

Rung 05

Calculus AB

Limits, derivatives, and integrals — worked problem by problem, with AP exam pacing in mind.

About Henry

Math clicks when someone slows down long enough to explain it right.

Henry has spent over 30 years teaching and tutoring math — in morning school, summer school, evening school, and virtual classrooms. He holds a BS in Mathematics from Barton College, a 7–12 mathematics teaching certification, and a Master's in Mathematics Education from Georgia State University, with additional coursework at Georgia Tech and UGA. Now a retired teacher, he tutors as often as he can.

Every session starts with the actual homework or test on the table — not a generic worksheet — and works backward from there on a shared digital whiteboard, until the concept holds on its own.
